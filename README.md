# Power BI Revenue Intelligence Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project is an end-to-end Power BI revenue intelligence solution designed to transform operational lead data into clear, actionable business insights.

The dashboard analyses lead generation, qualification, booking conversion, follow-up dependency and pipeline performance to help identify operational risks and opportunities for revenue improvement.

## Business Objectives

The solution was designed to answer key business questions including:

- How many leads are being generated?
- What proportion of leads are qualified?
- How effectively are qualified leads converting into bookings?
- How dependent is the pipeline on follow-up activity?
- How is performance changing over time?
- Where are the main operational risks and growth opportunities?

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Operational Intelligence

Provides an executive overview of lead volume, qualification, booking conversion, follow-up dependency and operational performance.

### 2. AI Performance Summary

Tracks performance over time, including lead volume, qualification rate and booking conversion trends.

### 3. Sales Funnel & Operational Actions

Examines progression through the sales funnel and highlights operational next actions.

### 4. Revenue Intelligence

Provides a strategic view of pipeline health, revenue performance and opportunities for business improvement.
